# Solesio Piedmontese tarot artwork

This folder contains the complete 78-card **Solesio Piedmontese tarot deck
(1865)** plus its matching reverse from Wikimedia Commons. The files were
downloaded at a display width of 500 px and retain the source colour.

- Complete 79-file image set (78 faces + one back):
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Piedmontese_tarot_deck_-_Solesio_-_1865>
- Collection: Bibliothèque nationale de France / Gallica
- Wikimedia Commons marks the selected files as public domain.

For semantic consistency with the app, Strength and Justice are stored under
their meaning-based app IDs rather than their historical printed numbers.
Images are displayed directly with no filter or recolouring.
