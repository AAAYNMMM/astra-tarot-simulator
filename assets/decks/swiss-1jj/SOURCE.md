# Swiss 1JJ tarot artwork

This folder contains the complete 78-card **Swiss 1JJ** deck collected in the
Wikimedia Commons Swiss Tarot category. The faces are stored at their original
available resolution or at a 500 px Commons preview width and retain the source
colour.

- Complete card category:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Swiss_Tarot>
- Historical design: Johann Georg Rauch, nineteenth century
- Wikimedia Commons marks the selected historical card images as public
  domain.
- `card-back.png` is an original reversible bitmap back generated for this
  simulator from the deck's red, blue, cream, and yellow palette. It is a
  standalone image, not a CSS filter applied to another back.

Swiss 1JJ traditionally substitutes Juno and Jupiter for the Popess and Pope.
Strength and Justice are stored under their meaning-based app IDs. Images are
displayed directly with no filter or recolouring.
