# Arnoult historical tarot artwork

This folder contains 78 public-domain card faces from the **1748 Arnoult
tarot**, a Besançon-pattern deck derived from the Tarot de Marseille tradition.
The files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons at a display width of 500 px
and keep the colour of the source images.

- Complete card category:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Tarot_de_Marseille_(Single_Cards)>
- Collection: Bibliothèque nationale de France / Gallica
- Compact face files: `TT Tarot.png`, `T1 Tarot.png` … and the four suits
- Matching reverse scan:
  `Jeu de tarot miniature, dit "tarot Arnoult" … (002 of 156).jpg`
- Wikimedia Commons marks the selected files as public domain.

For semantic consistency with the app, Strength and Justice are stored under
their meaning-based app IDs rather than their historical printed numbers.
Images are displayed directly with no filter or recolouring.
